# Info

## :material-calendar: When

- We have provisionally set the date for the 9th - 11th of April, 2027.

## :material-map-marker: Where

- We are hoping to hold the event at the new home of [East Essex Hackspace](https://eehack.space/) - 
 [The Freight House](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZZhvovmc6GXhhVH47) in Rochford, Essex.

## :material-ticket: Tickets

- Tickets aren't currently for sale. We'll announce them once things are a bit more organised.

## :material-map-marker-path: Travel

### :material-train: Train

- Rochford station is a stones throw away, which is on the [Shenfield to Southend Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenfield%E2%80%93Southend_line).  
- Direct trains run from London Liverpool Street, and take approximately 50 minutes.

### :material-car: Car

- The venue is approximately 30 minutes away from Junction 29 on the M25.  
- There is an on-site pay and display car park (rates TBC)
- Loading is permitted for X minutes (TBC)

### :material-airplane: Plane

- ["London" Southend Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Southend_Airport) is one stop away on the train.
- Destinations served include Belfast, Edinburgh, Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin, Munich, and many of the popular holiday islands.
- Alternatively you can charter a [Private Jet](https://www.westonaviation.com/charter/)
